# Ragvia product assets

These are unchanged copies of the user-approved Ragvia identity from
`web/public/brand/explorations/ragvia/`, promoted to a stable product path.
The original exploration and reproducible brand sources remain in place.

- `logo.svg` and `logo-inverse.svg`: outlined symbol and lowercase wordmark, 306 × 96.
- `mark.svg` and `mark-inverse.svg`: standalone convergence symbol, 64 × 64.
- `favicon.svg`: optically adjusted 16px mark with system dark-mode adaptation.
- PNG app icons and the maskable SVG retain their original padding.

Product imports use `web/lib/brand.ts` and `web/components/brand-logo.tsx`.
The inverse logo follows the application's selected dark mode.
